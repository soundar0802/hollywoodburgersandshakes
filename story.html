ABOUT Us

Welcome to Hollywood Burgers & Shakes -a growing food brand built around great food, great service and an experience our customers love.

What started as a single location has grown into an exciting and expanding business, with five sites across the UK and more locations on the way.

Our aim is simple: to build a recognisable brand that brings delicious food, quality ingredients and a welcoming atmosphere to communities across the country.

As we continue to grow, we remain focused on delivering the same great Hollywood experience at every location.

Our Story

Our story began in Manchester, where the first Hollywood Burgers & Shakes location was established.

From there, we continued to grow and expanded into Edinburgh, building on the success of the brand and developing a loyal customer base.

Today, Hollywood Burgers & Shakes has five sites in total, with further expansion already planned.

We are proud of how far we have come -but this is only the beginning. Our vision is to continue growing Hollywood Burgers & Shakes into a recognised and successful brand across the UK.
